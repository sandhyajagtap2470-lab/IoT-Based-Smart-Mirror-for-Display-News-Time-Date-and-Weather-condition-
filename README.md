# IoT-Based-Smart-Mirror-for-Display-News-Time-Date-and-Weather-condition-
In today’s modern world, smart technologies are becoming an essential part 
of everyday life. The Internet of Things (IOT) allows different devices to connect 
and communicate through the internet, making systems more intelligent, efficient, 
and convenient. Smart home technologies are rapidly gaining popularity because 
they help automate daily activities and improve the overall quality of life.
 A Smart Mirror is an innovative device that combines the functionality of a 
traditional mirror with digital technology. It uses electronic components such as a 
microcontroller, display units, sensors, and internet connectivity to provide real-
time information while still working as a normal mirror. The mirror can display 
useful information such as weather updates, news headlines, and system 
messages directly on the mirror surface.
 The proposed Smart Mirror with AI Device Controller integrates IOT
technology to provide both information display and voice-controlled home 
automation. The ESP32 microcontroller acts as the central controller of the 
system and connects to the internet through Wi-Fi. The system retrieves real-time 
information such as weather data and news updates and displays them on the 
OLED and LCD screens mounted behind the mirror.
 In addition, the system uses a VC-02 voice recognition module that allows 
users to control multiple electrical appliances using voice commands. The ESP32 
processes these commands and operates the relay driver module to turn devices 
like lights, fans, or other appliances ON or OFF.
